## English (en-US, default)

**App name** (28): Buzzle: Cozy Bee Hive Puzzle

**Short description** (78): Grow a cozy bee hive, tend a flower garden and solve a daily honeycomb puzzle.

**Full description:**

Buzzle is a cozy little game about bees, honey and a hexagonal puzzle. There are no ads, no purchases and no account. Just your hive.

🍯 YOUR HIVE
• Build up to 19 honeycombs and upgrade each one up to level 40
• Bees keep making honey while the app is closed, up to your storage limit
• Four hive upgrades: Worker Bees, Wax Pantry, Flower Meadow and Queen's Chamber

🧩 HONEYCOMB PUZZLE
• Drag across 3 or more neighbouring cells of one colour
• Chains of 6+ leave a royal-jelly bomb, rainbow jokers and a combo multiplier for long chains
• Daily puzzle: the same board for everyone each day, 20 moves, 1–3 stars and a day streak
• Weekend puzzle with its own stars and rewards
• Free play with boosters, as many rounds as you like
• A Hint button whenever you get stuck

🌻 FLOWER GARDEN
• Plant sunflowers, clover, lavender, cornflowers and mint, plus rare flowers
• Water the beds: flowers grow only while the soil is wet
• Flowers give nectar and boost your honey. Plant the colour of the day for ×1.5 points in the puzzle

🐝 13 BEES TO COLLECT
• Every bee has its own permanent bonus
• Level bees up to 10, rename them and dress them in scarves, bows, glasses, berets and more
• Watch them fly around the hive, land on the combs and swarm when you collect honey

🎁 SURPRISE COMBS AND DAILY GOALS
• Surprise combs are earned by playing only, never sold. The odds are shown in the game
• Three daily tasks, a weekly chest, a 7-day login calendar and a streak freeze
• Decorations for the hive and the garden
• Seasonal events with their own rewards

✨ ALSO
• Optional daily reminders (local notifications)
• Classic and Midnight themes, several comb styles
• English, Ukrainian and Russian
• Export your save as a text code and import it on another device

Your progress stays on your device. Buzzle is a small hobby project by one developer. Feedback is very welcome!
